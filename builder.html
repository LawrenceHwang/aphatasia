Build your own palace

The best custom palace is a sequence you already know without trying: your commute, your gym routine, making your usual dinner, a walk you've taken a hundred times. Write one story beat per hook. Never describe how things look — describe what they sound like, feel like, smell like, what you do, or what happens next because of it.

Chain rule: every beat should connect to the next by sequence (“then”) or cause (“so”). If you can ask “why did I move on?” and the story answers, the chain will recall itself.
