What is a non-visual Story Palace?

A classic memory palace asks you to “see” vivid cartoons in imagined rooms. Useless if your mind's eye is blank. A Story Palace replaces mental pictures with a fixed sequence of numbered hooks — anchored by real physical micro-gestures (Enactment Effect), blindfolded spatial coordinates, rhyme pegs, or cause-and-effect chains. You bind each item to a hook with one causal or rule-based sentence, and our live coach catches passive “visual traps” as you type.

1
head ✋ touch/feel

You wake and lift your head — press a palm flat on top of it and feel the warmth there.

Enact it: Press a palm flat on top of your head as you say your sentence aloud.

your item “Buy olive oil” → “Because I press my head, the slippery olive oil bottle slides off and forces me to catch it.”

2
light switch ⌖ position

Immediately inside on the right wall, shoulder height: your hand finds the light switch.

zero-imagery rule “Opportunity cost” → “Flipping the light switch on automatically turns the fan off — choosing one forces giving up the other.”

The science this is built on

  • Aphantasia is real — and often multisensory. About 0.9% report no voluntary visual imagery (~3% very dim; Frontiers in Psychology, n=9,063, 2024), and most aphantasic people experience reductions in other internal senses too (Dawes et al., Scientific Reports, 2020; Dawes, Keogh & Pearson, Neuroscience Research, 2024). That's why our self-check maps multiple senses and offers a full Zero-Imagery (Logic + Action) lens.
  • Spatial coordinates stay intact without visual pictures. Drawing and psychophysical studies reveal a clean split between object imagery (reduced) and spatial positioning (intact) (Bainbridge et al., Cortex, 2021). The Room Grid palace and the Lukasa Spatial Map directly harness this intact spatial channel.
  • Semantic, verbal, and compensatory strategies excel. Aphantasic students achieve equal or better grades using semantic networks, condensed inner speech, explanation generation, and external recoding (Frontiers in Psychology, 2025; “Unseen strategies”, New Ideas in Psychology, 2025).
  • Embodied enactment, speaking aloud, and simple symbols beat mental photos. Performing a real micro-gesture (Enactment Effect), speaking answers aloud (Production Effect), and using clean geometric glyphs rather than rich photos (Journal of Vision, 2025) all work without imagery — echoed by aphantasic memory researcher Lynne Kelly's physical lukasa boards (Art of Memory).
  • Spaced retrieval & elaborative interrogation lock memories in. Built-in Leitner spacing, bidirectional recall, progressive cue fading, and instant repair of missed links put the highest-utility cognitive-science methods to work in your browser.

This site is an educational tool, not a medical instrument. The self-check is not a diagnosis.
