How does your mind actually encode things?

Three minutes, fourteen questions. Aphantasia is not one-size-fits-all: research using multi-sensory questionnaires shows many people without a mind's eye also lack imagined sound (anauralia), smell, or touch — while their spatial layout, logic, and physical enactment stay rock-solid. Your answers stay in this browser and automatically tailor the prompts and palaces across this site. This is a self-reflection tool, not a diagnosis.
